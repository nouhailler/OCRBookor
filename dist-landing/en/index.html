Documentary Assessment

Why a scanned PDF is not enough

A PDF file composed only of raw raster images is inert: text cannot be searched or copied, hyphenation is broken, and e-readers cannot reflow the layout.

Inert & Invisible Text

Text cannot be selected, copied, or indexed by a search engine. Screen readers remain blind.

Skew & Optical Noise

Pages suffer from rotation skew, binding gutter curve distortion, and paper aging foxing stains.

Broken Hyphens & Punctuation

Words split at line ends remain broken, and typographical rules (guillemets, non-breaking spaces) are lost.

The OCRBookor Solution

DPI calculation, automatic deskewing, Otsu binarization, local Tesseract v5, contextual dehyphenation, and strict EPUB 3 export.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is OCRBookor?

A local software suite to transform scanned PDF books into structured EPUB 3, Markdown, and HTML documents with a dual-pane reconciliation engine.

Are my books uploaded to the internet?

No. OCRBookor runs 100% locally and offline via Tesseract v5 WebAssembly or native CLI. Your data never leaves your computer.

Does the software read handwriting?

No, OCRBookor v1.3.0 is designed for movable-type lead-set and contemporary printed books.

How do I install the landing page as a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

Click the "Install PWA" button in the top navigation bar (on Chrome, Edge, or Android). On iOS (iPhone/iPad), tap Safari's share button and select "Add to Home Screen". The landing page will then be accessible even without an internet connection thanks to its dedicated Service Worker.
